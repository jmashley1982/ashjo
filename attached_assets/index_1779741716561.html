ABOUT

ASH, IN THE WILD
BASEMENT, 3:12 AM, 2025

Ash Johansen is a character and pseudonym birthed from the mind of veteran Texas artist, producer, musician, and singer, Jason M. Ashley. After a multi‑year attempt to find a versatile pop vocalist, Jason gave up — then AI music happened. Taking hours of incomplete demos, he turned his music and own voice into Ash Johansen. Fierce, fun, and fucked up. The music is unmistakably Jason, but Ash adds that perfect edge: sweet and pleasing, but raw and unforgiving.
